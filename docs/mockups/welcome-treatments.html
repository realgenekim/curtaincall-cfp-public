Welcome page — main-column treatments

All three: the "Events / Every event you run…" header, "Start AIE replay demo", and top "Create event" button are GONE (the door cards and rail already carry those acts). "signed in as … Log out" stays. The lonely right column is replaced by live proof — submissions only, no ratings, no coverage (insider metrics a newcomer can't parse; a big number + a real agenda link is proof anyone gets).

W1 — Doors, then a proof strip

Single column. After the three doors: "Live on Curtain Call right now" as a row of compact cards — works for two or three events, never looks lonely. My recommendation: calmest reading order — who you are, what you can do, proof it's real, why we built it.
