How do you rate a talk from the row?

Three treatments of the same ledger row. The question: after we removed the ▸, what invites a reviewer to rate inline? Hover things to feel them. My verdict is on each.

A — the Rate pill (recommended)

The YOU column stops being a dash and becomes a button: gold-outlined ☆ Rate when you haven't, marker-yellow 3.5 when you have. Click → the controls row opens beneath. Obvious, compact, and the affordance IS the empty state.
