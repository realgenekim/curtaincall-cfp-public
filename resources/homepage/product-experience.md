## See the whole call, not a canned tour

Start as the organizer. Open the CFP, shape the form, bring in the programming committee, review hundreds of proposals, decide without accidentally notifying anyone, build the schedule, and publish the program. That is the job Curtain Call is here to make calm.

The other roles are not costumes worn by the same superuser:

- **A reviewer** gets the talks and shared conversation they need — not event settings, communications, or publishing.
- **A speaker** gets their own proposal, profile, tasks, files, and schedule — not the conference back office.

Those boundaries are part of the product. `curtaincallcfp.com` is the durable system running our real Enterprise AI Summit call, and its history is meant to last. Its demo controls create clearly labeled simulations alongside real work so a new organizer never faces an empty screen. For the judging walkthrough, a separate resettable sandbox lets you cross the same season as organizer, reviewer, and speaker — including hundreds of submissions accelerated through a time machine — without putting a live conference at risk.
